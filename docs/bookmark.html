Ranking bookmark

One click on a Top War ranking screen adds Rank, Name, Points and today's date to the Game rankings sheet.

1 · Install (once, on a computer)

Drag this onto your bookmarks bar:

2698 Extract

No bookmarks bar? Press Ctrl+Shift+B (Mac: Cmd+Shift+B) to show it. Can't drag? , then make a new bookmark and paste it as the URL.

2 · Use

  1. Open the Top War web game and the ranking you want.
  2. Scroll to the bottom of the list once — the game only loads about 30 rows until you do.
  3. Click 2698 Extract in the bookmarks bar, check the count, and press Send to sheet.

Each send is added below the lists already in the sheet, dated by the game's day (UTC). The bookmark only reads what the game has already loaded on your screen: it sends nothing to the game and taps nothing.
